---
title: I remembered the day I realised you were different
created: 2025-05-09T21:40:00.000Z
---

I remember the day i realised you were
different, that you were like me in some way i
can't quite identify, it felt like we recognised
something in each other in that moment, A
playful, combative spirit maybe. That familiar,
mischievous sparkle in your eye like I had
finally met one of my kind...
You are remarkably strong-willed and sure of
yourself, so confident and comfortable in
your own skin, i find that inspiring still now,
you were magical like my life changed, it was
different from any other thing..
