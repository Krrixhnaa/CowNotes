---
title: Into my heart an air that kills from yon
created: 2025-10-29T18:40:00.000Z
---

Into my heart an air that kills from yon,
Far country blows: what are those blue
remembered hills, what farms, what spires
are those ? that is the land of lost content, I
see it shining plain, the happy highway where
i went and cannot go again...
