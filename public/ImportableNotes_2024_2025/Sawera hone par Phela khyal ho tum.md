title: Sawera hone par Phela khyal ho tum
created: 2024-10-14T21:15:00.000Z
---

Sawera hone par Phela khyal ho tum
dophere mein jo khud se puchta hu, wo pyara
sawal ho tum.
Sham dhalte-dhalte mein tumhari yadoo
mein, kho sa jatta hun
Aur raat ke sapno mein tumhara ho jata hu.
Aur dua karta hu
Jishe sirf mein pad saku wo kitab ban jao
Jo sirf mujhe ae wo khwab ban jao
Jispe sirf Mera adhikar ho wo gulab ban jao
Mehfil mein baat kar saku wo baat ban jao
Jo sirf mere sang gujre wo raat ban jao
Aur Jo sirf mujhe chadhe wo jamh ban jao
Ek khwaish hai ki
Mere Chote se dil ke mehman ban jao
Jishe zindagi bhar karu wo kaam ban jao
Sach kahu, tho meri jaan ban jao!
