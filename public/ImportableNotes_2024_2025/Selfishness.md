Selfishness
I hope the war doesn't start today, there is a
coffee shop I need to take a friend i have
known for too long to, where they sell the
best sandwiches, this side of the river hope
they wait till Tuesday or tomorrow, at least if
there's is a singular god furious at our selfish
squabbling and he sent these bombs in the
post may the postman go on strike so I can
tell my lover I love her, so child can eat, so my
mother can dance one more, my father can
decide he loves me but if war starts today, a
part of me won't be opposed, as the
billionaire burns with his Pinocchio nose, the
kindling of this fire started long before, bcz
the flames kiss the rich, they have already
swallowed the poor
