---
title: And when death finally finds me, I hope it
created: 2026-02-14T21:46:00.000Z
---

And when death finally finds me, I hope it
doesn't come as a stranger. I hope it leans
close and whispers, "Come now... the fight is
over."
I hope it takes my hand gently,
like an old friend who knows my name, who
understands why my bones are tired and my
heart has learned to ache quietly.
I hope it says, "You've carried more than your
share. You don't have to be strong anymore.
Let me hold this weight for you."
I hope it promises me a place where the noise
cannot follow, where nothing asks and nothing
hurts.
A place where I am not broken, only finally still.
A place where it whispers, softly, "Here, there is
quiet. Here... you may rest."
