For a long time now, every meeting with
another human being has been a
collision. I feel too much, sense too
much, am exhausted by the
reverberations after even the simplest
conversation. But the deep collision is
and has been with my unregenerate,
tormenting, and tormented self. I have
written every poem, evezy novel, for the
same purpose-to find out what I think, to
know where I stand. I am unable to
become what I see. I feel like an
inadequate machine, a machine that
breaks down at crucial moments, grinds
to a dreadful halt, "won't go," or, even
woise,explodes in some innocent
person's face.
