---
title: When morning breaks, you are my very first
created: 2025-12-29T13:54:00.000Z
---

When morning breaks, you are my very first
thought.
By afternoon, you become the tender question
I quietly ask myself.
As evening slowly settles,
I lose myself in memories of you,
And in the dreams of the night,
I become wholly yours.
And so I prayyy,
Become the book only I am allowed to read.
Become the dream that comes only to me.
Become the rose that belongs to no one but my
hands.
Become the conversation I can share in a
crowded room.
Become the night that passes only beside me.
Become the wine that intoxicates only my soul.
I hold one wish close to my heart—
Become a guest in my small, fragile heart.
Become the purpose I choose for a lifetime.
And if I speak the truth...
Become my life itself.
