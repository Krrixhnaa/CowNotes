think, one day, when I'm better, 11m going to miss
some of the beautiful things I can't see right now
because I'm hurting too much. Like when I'm making
coffee and it's raining outside and I have a book in
mind which reads like it could be my new favourite, or
when I'm cuddling my blankets, lights turned off,
music in the background, and I'm half-listening, half-
thinking about when I was in third grade and my dad
read me a funny book about numbers to make me like
math. It's infuriating, really: to be nostalgic for the
present tense, to feel the loss of what hasn't even left
yet, because I don t feel alive enough to be happy
with it yet. I'm trying to learn how to stay in a moment
without mentally packing it away for later, for when
I'm better. or happier. The other day I went for a walk
and saw the sky looking like God's canvas of pink and
orange and blue, and it almost made me believe
again. I reached for my phone, thought "this'll look
nice later," but I let it stay unphotographed. Maybe
not every beautiful thing needs to be rescued fic time.
Some things deserve to disappear right on schedule.
And I deserve to
be happy with it right now. I don't deserve to store it
away for a hypothetical future in which I'm smiling.
Sometimes I think that's what growing up really is -
slowly becoming tware of how temporary everything
is, and trying to love it anyway.
