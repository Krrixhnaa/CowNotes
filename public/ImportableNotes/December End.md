December End
December arrives, 2025 is ending and
something inside me is ending too.
I don't want to be someone's favourite
anymore.
I don't want drama fake faces or
emotionally immature people around me.
I'm not looking for new friends.
I'm not looking for a partner.
People who never valued me don't get a
single inch of space now.
As this year closes there's only one
person I left
to fix things with "MYSELF"
The same self I've ignored for years.
The same self I've hurt more than
anyone else
ever could.
2025 is ending
and so is my desire to be loved by
someone else...
