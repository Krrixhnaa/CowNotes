created: 2025-11-07T20:39:00.000Z
---

I notice everything And by everything, I
literally mean everything. I notice when
someone stops calling me up like they
used to. I notice when the way someone
talks to me starts changing. I notice the
little things that people do, and the little
things they used to do. I notice when
things change and when it's no longer
the same. I notice every single little
detail. I just don't say anything.
