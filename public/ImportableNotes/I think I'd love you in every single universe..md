---
title: I think I'd love you in every single universe.
created: 2026-02-25T16:54:00.000Z
---

I think I'd love you in every single universe.
My hands just know yours too well.
I've memorized your touch, every inch of your
skin.
In every life, we'd find each other again and
again.
If you were a flower, I'd be a bee.
If I was a fish, you'd be the salt in the sea.
You're always right with me, wherever I am.
On the beach, I'd see you in every grain of
sand.
That beach stretched for miles, and I'd walk for
days.
I'd study the shore to love you in all sorts of
ways.
I'd count every grain of sand, if that's what you
wanted.
And if you were a ghost, then I'd pray my house
was haunted.
