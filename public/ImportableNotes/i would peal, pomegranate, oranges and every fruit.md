i would peal, pomegranate, oranges and every fruit
you ask me to, if it proves how much I love you. I
would always Tilt the umbrella a bit towards you, in
the rain even if I would be soaking wet. And if ever
you doubt my love for you I would write thousands of
love books for you, even if it means bleeding on half
of them. I can let my soul taint, if it can keep yours
pure. I can destroy my world just to build yours
