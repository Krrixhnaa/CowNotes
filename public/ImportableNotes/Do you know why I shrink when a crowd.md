Do you know why I shrink when a crowd
forms? Why I give up my seat for
someone else to sit? Why I order the
cheapest thing on the menu when
someone else is paying? Why I give
away the last piece of the cookie even
when I wanted it the most? ltls because
I've spent my whole life trying to take up
less space. Because somewhere along
the way i convinced myself that existing
too loudly was a burden. And so, I
learned to fade to exist quietly, gently,
almost invisibly...
