Some shit i care
Dudes be likeee. Oh, I would die for you. Oh, I
would kill for you. 0K, well, I would be kind for
you. I would reject the impulses to indulge in my
violent nature for you. I would be here for you,
no matter what it takes. I would keep the light
on for you until you felt safe, and I would hold
you so tight until you felt at peace. Sure, I can
be strong if I have to. I can fight if it's truly
necessary. But when I think about the way that
I love you, it does not make me feel violent. It
makes me feel quiet and gentle and calm.
When I look at you, I know that I'm not a violent
dog and I will not bite.
