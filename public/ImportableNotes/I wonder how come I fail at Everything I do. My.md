I wonder how come I fail at Everything I do. My
hobbies were never good enough. My grades
never met the expectations, I was never pretty
enough. Always the disappointment. I guess I
never tried enough. But in my memory I can still
see vividly how hard I tried. How my hands
were shaking from exhaustion, how I had eye
fatigue due to lack of sleep but ofcourse I never
tried enough.
