Imma LOFE
looser of fucking everything
i cover my face a lot these days. it's this quiet,
unbearable discomfort. like i'm trying to hide
something, but the something is me, and it's
broken. i look at myself and feel this sinking
disappointment. is this all i am? is this who i've
always been? and the feeling never really
leaves. it just sits there. i can't remember the
last time i looked at myself and felt even a
trace of okay. not proud. not confident. just...
okay. everything turns into a list: not pretty
enough, not smart enough, not strong enough.
just... never enough.
