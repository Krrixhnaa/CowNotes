---
title: As a final act of love I'll let you leave without ever
created: 2026-09-17T15:03:00.000Z
---

As a final act of love I'll let you leave without ever
knowing how bad it hurts. I'll memorize the sound of
the door closing and pretend it didn't echo through
my ribs. I'll keep answering "I'm fine" with a throat full
of glass because you don't need to carry my grief too.
You get to start over with clean hands. I'll stay here
with the stain of you on everything, learning how to
breathe around the hollow you carved out. If loving
you means bleeding quietly so you can heal, then I'll
do it. God. I'll do it.
