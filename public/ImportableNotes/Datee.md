Datee
I have a date with Jane today.
All is ready. Morning waits.
We walk together to the church,
quiet steps, shared silence.
Afterward, coffee warms our hands,
cake is broken, sweet and soft
in a small café.
We wander through the park,
leaves listening to our thoughts.
Then the aquarium—
dolphins turning joy into water,
penguins, her favorites,
moving like gentle laughter.
After lunch, we rest.
Time loosens its grip.
"So what did we do this morning?"
we ask, smiling,
and speak of it again and again
until memory blurs,
until remembering feels unnecessary.
Sleep finds us.
And the night holds us
back at the church,
where the day began.
