Oh, to run away and ghost everyone, just
disappear without a trace, delete all
social media, change number, start a
new life in a beachside countryside, and
live the life I've always wanted! And if,
one day, someone seeks my presence,
may they, find me in the company of the
unseen, where the crowds are fewer, the
sky is blue, and the wind is free..
