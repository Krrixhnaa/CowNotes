title: When I open my soul to you it will not be
created: 2025-12-04T15:45:00.000Z
---

When I open my soul to you it will not be
blooming with roses, it will be raw, it will
tremble with every word that leaves my
mouth, and it will be messy. You must
understand, that love for me has always
been an act of surrender, Every
confession, every truth offer, comes with
the risk of bleeding. I'm selflessly
handing you the dagger that is designed
to kill me. I do not sharpen it, only place
it in your hands and hope you will not
strike
